# Code of conduct

HL7Lens is a small project for people who work with healthcare interfaces. Keep every issue, discussion and
pull request respectful, on topic and useful.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- **Never post real patient data**, credentials, internal hostnames or anything your employer would not want
  public. Use synthetic messages or a reviewed *De-identified copy*.
- No spam or advertising.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove content and block accounts that break these rules. To report a
problem privately, use **Report a vulnerability** on the Security tab or contact the maintainer through
GitHub.
